Identifying Outliers
Interquartile Range
IQR=q 3 −q 1
Whisker Interval
[q1 −1.5(IQR),q 3 + 1.5(IQR)]
Observations outside this interval are classified as outliers
The 1.5×IQRrule is a standard heuristic
Important Reminder
Outliers are not automatically errors; they may represent important rare events.

Drawing Whiskers
Important Clarification
Whiskers extend only to actual observed data values.
Upper whisker: Largest observation satisfying
x≤q 3 + 1.5(IQR)
Lower whisker: Smallest observation satisfying
x≥q 1 −1.5(IQR)
Property
Whiskers never extend beyond observed data points.
