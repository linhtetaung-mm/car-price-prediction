Population vs. Randomization Models
Population Model
Subjects are random samples from a large
population.
Goal: Generalize conclusions to the broader
population.
Common in survey statistics.
Randomization Model
Subjects are fixed.
Randomness comes from treatment assignment.
Common in clinical trials.
Nonparametric methods are valid under both inference frameworks.

Permutation Testing
Modern computers allow exact permutation tests by evaluating every possible labeling of the data.
No reliance on Normal approximations.

Summary
Nonparametric methods avoid strong Normality assumptions.
Rank-based procedures reduce sensitivity to outliers.
Signed Rank Tests analyze paired samples.
Rank Sum Tests analyze independent samples.
Randomization and permutation ideas provide strong inferential foundations.
Always interpret both:
Statistical significance,
Practical significance.
